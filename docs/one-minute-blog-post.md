# Chief Architect / AI Architect - Two roles. Same business domains. Different questions.

A Chief Architect and a Chief AI Architect share the business. They do not share the dashboard.

The Chief Architect is accountable for the path. 

Can work move from one business service to the next — and are the calculations on that path accurate? 

In FOREX that is trades, matching, reconciliation, the trade book, inter-bank events, and intra-day / mid-day / end-of-day P/L. 

In a healthcare provider it is encounters, fulfillment of care, eligibility and orders-to-results, census, and utilization clocks. 

In eCommerce it is order, fulfillment, activation, notification, billing.

The Chief AI Architect sits above that path. The question is not “did the journey complete?” It is: did AI make or support the right decision, on current evidence, inside approved authority, while the model was actually up, at a cost worth the outcome?

Same three domains. Different object of attention. A mid-day hedge recommendation. An order suggestion in the encounter. A hold-or-release at checkout. One decision inside the pipe — not another journey map.

If your AI dashboard looks like trades → execution → settlement, it has slipped back into the Chief Architect view.
